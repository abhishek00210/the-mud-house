Your stay

How do I check room availability?

Choose a room and send an enquiry with your preferred dates and guest details. The team will confirm availability and share the current rates. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Which room is right for my visit?

Explore Deluxe, Premium, Family and Dormitory room concepts, then share your group size and requirements with the team to discuss a suitable option.

Can I enquire about a group stay?

Yes. Select a group enquiry on the contact page and tell us your dates, group size and room preferences so the team can discuss availability and arrangements.

Dining

Can I dine without staying at the hotel?

Non-resident dining arrangements are to be confirmed. Contact the team before planning your visit.

How can I discuss dietary requirements?

Share your dietary requirements with the team before ordering or requesting a table. Ingredients, preparation and suitability must be confirmed by the restaurant.

Experiences

Is accommodation included in every package?

Not necessarily. Some experience concepts include a stay while others are day experiences. Inclusions and arrangements require confirmation for each package.

When is an experience booking confirmed?

The team will discuss availability, proposed arrangements, a quote and the applicable booking conditions with you. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a booking.
